# Slop Shield: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-26_

Slop Shield runs entirely in your browser. It has no server, no account, no analytics, and no ads.

**What it stores, on your device only (chrome.storage.local):**
- Your settings (on/off, hide or blur, which rules are enabled).
- Channels and shops you block, channels you mark "Not AI", and channels it learned carry YouTube's "Made with AI" label.
- A cache of per-video results (the video ID and whether it was labelled), so it doesn't check the same video twice.
- A daily counter that caps how many pages it checks.

**What leaves your device:** nothing is sent to us or to any third party. To read YouTube's own "Made with AI" label, the extension requests the public YouTube watch page for videos on your screen, directly from your browser to youtube.com, exactly as if you had opened them. Those requests go only to youtube.com. No browsing data is collected, sold or shared.

**Permissions:** `storage` holds the data above. Access to `youtube.com` lets it hide videos there.

**Removing your data:** uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored.

Questions or problems: open an issue at https://github.com/saikumargangi/slop-shield/issues
